# Glucose Meal Coach

A personal meal tool based on the 5 glucose hacks from Jessie Inchauspé (Glucose Goddess): build a meal, see the best eating order, get ideas to shrink the spike, and log your glucose readings, feelings and photos.

**Open on your phone:** https://thesnagtag-coder.github.io/glucose-meal-coach/

Then use your browser menu: **Add to Home screen** (Android: Chrome menu ⋮ - Add to Home screen / Install app; iPhone: Safari Share - Add to Home Screen).

- Your meals, readings and photos are saved only on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.
- Works offline after the first visit.
- Meal from a photo opens the Claude page, which sends the meal back with "Send to my phone app".

Not medical advice. If you take diabetes medication, follow your doctor's targets.
